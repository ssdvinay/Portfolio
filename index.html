Enterprise Automation
& Intelligence Platform

A confidential B2B SaaS ecosystem designed to unify RPA, AI document processing, business intelligence reporting, and organizational governance — transforming fragmented enterprise operations into one strategic command center.

Role
UI/UX Designer
Type
Enterprise SaaS
Platform
Web Application
Note
Confidential — Name Anonymized
The Problem

Enterprise Operations Challenge

Large organizations struggled with operational inefficiencies from disconnected systems, repetitive manual workflows, and fragmented data governance. Critical processes like order-to-cash, procurement approvals, and executive reporting were scattered across multiple tools and spreadsheets.

Manual order entry from emails into SAP — hours lost daily

No centralized KPI dashboards for Sales, Finance & Procurement

Technical barriers blocking business user adoption of automation

Validation gaps creating compliance and governance risks

Fragmented client, customer & user data across systems

Zero visibility into automation performance and error rates

Users

4 Core Personas

01
Business Analyst
Monitors KPIs, builds automation scenarios, improves operational efficiency. Needs accessible tools — not IT-grade complexity.
02
Operations Manager
Processes customer orders faster, reduces errors, handles validation bottlenecks. Needs OCR + SAP automation without technical overhead.
03
Enterprise Administrator
Manages clients, controls permissions, ensures compliance across a multi-client organizational hierarchy.
04
Executive Leadership
Needs strategic visibility, boardroom-ready reporting, and cost efficiency insights — not raw data.
Platform Design

Core Modules

01
Reporting & Intelligence Module
Strategic dashboards for Sales, Finance, Procurement & Inventory — dynamic KPI cards, advanced filters, configurable charts, role-based data visibility.
02
RPA Scenario Builder
No-code / low-code automation canvas — progressive node execution, OAuth integrations (Outlook, SAP, Salesforce), OCR + NLP extraction, and AI JSON transformation.
03
Action Center (Human-in-Loop)
Failed validations pause automation and trigger human review tasks. Correct and resume — maintaining full audit trail without breaking the flow.
04
Organization & Client Governance
Layered hierarchy: Organization → Clients → Customers. Side-modal onboarding, stepper registration, master data, and multi-client context switching.
05
User Management & Permissions
Role-based access control, power user setup, admin governance, and security hierarchy across all modules and clients.
Key UX Decisions

Design Solutions

Progressive Execution Model: Designed a step-by-step run mode — configure one node, test it, validate success, then expand. Reduces cognitive overload and builds confidence for non-technical business users.

Role-Based Information Architecture: Executives, analysts, and operations teams see only what's contextually relevant — implemented through progressive disclosure and role-specific entry points.

Modular Dashboard Design: Data-dense enterprise dashboards designed with KPI hierarchy, dashboard switching, custom filters, and configurable chart views — strategic clarity without visual clutter.

02 — AI Platform · Industrial SaaS · Web + Mobile

AssetPulse AI

Enterprise-grade AI-powered web & mobile platform for predictive, preventive, and reactive asset maintenance — centralizing equipment lifecycle, spare parts inventory, and SAP/ERP integration for industrial operations at scale.

Role
UI/UX Designer
Platforms
Web + Mobile
Industry
Manufacturing · Logistics · Energy
Focus
Dashboard UX · Mobile Workflows · Industrial Design
The Problem

Industrial Maintenance Challenge

Industrial maintenance teams relied on disconnected spreadsheets, manual logs, and purely reactive servicing. Equipment failures surprised teams, spare parts were unavailable, and siloed ERP systems created costly delays and data errors across operations.

Unplanned equipment downtime with no advance warning

Fragmented asset records with no central registry

Inventory shortages delaying critical repairs

Manual SAP/ERP entry causing delays and errors

No failure prediction before actual equipment breakdown

High costs from inefficient maintenance scheduling

My Role

Design Scope

Led the end-to-end product experience across both web and mobile platforms — from research and information architecture through high-fidelity design and design system delivery.

Product ResearchCompetitor BenchmarkingJourney MappingInformation ArchitectureDashboard UX StrategyMobile-First WorkflowsHigh-Fidelity UIDesign SystemSAP Integration UX
Platform Design

Core Modules

01
Centralized Equipment Registry
Unified asset database — Equipment ID, installation history, maintenance records, warranty lifecycle, and ERP-linked data in one searchable interface.
02
Reactive Maintenance Module
Immediate issue logging and work order assignment. Designed for speed — minimum taps for urgent breakdown response, even in field conditions with gloves on.
03
Preventive Maintenance Scheduling
Automated recurring schedules based on time intervals, usage thresholds, machine runtime, and compliance requirements. Reduced missed servicing significantly.
04
Predictive Maintenance (AI-Driven)
Historical data analysis forecasts failures — performance anomaly detection, downtime risk scoring, and maintenance recommendations before breakdowns occur.
05
Spare Parts Inventory Management
Visual stock thresholds, auto reorder triggers, vendor linkage, and stockout prevention — making critical inventory states impossible to miss at a glance.
06
SAP & ERP Integration
Seamless sync of equipment master data, purchase orders, inventory updates, and maintenance logs. Eliminated all manual data entry requirements.
Design Approach

Web & Mobile Strategy

Web Platform — Built for Managers: Focused on maintenance dashboards, asset tracking, inventory controls, predictive analytics, and KPI visibility. Information hierarchy was rigorously enforced — most critical data always surfaces first.

Mobile Platform — Built for Technicians: Optimized for field conditions — one-tap task updates, simplified forms, QR/barcode asset identification, real-time updates, push notifications, and offline-friendly usability for low-connectivity environments.
